BEFORE THE FIRST SHEET

A small test. A perfect fit.

  1. Measure & save

    Enter the sheet size, margins and gaps. Confirm measurements, then save.

  2. Check on plain paper

    Download the alignment sheet. Print on paper cut to the same size and compare against your labels under light. Its reference line must measure 50 mm.

  3. Print at actual size

    In the Epson Windows driver, create a User-Defined paper size matching your sheet. In your PDF viewer choose Actual size / 100%, single-sided, one page per sheet. Disable Fit, scaling and borderless expansion.

  4. Try one label sheet

    Load one sheet at a time, printable side up in the L300 rear feeder. Check alignment and ink drying before the full batch. Use stock suitable for inkjet printing.

Uniform shift? Adjust Move right / down. Increasing drift across rows or columns? Recheck gaps and print scaling. Text should stay at least 3 mm from each sheet edge.
